## Overview

Naruto is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

## The series

The franchise consists of two main parts:

- **Naruto (2002-2007)** - Follows Naruto's journey as a young ninja learning the ways of the shinobi
- **Naruto Shippuden (2007-2017)** - Continues the story with an older Naruto facing greater threats

## Key concepts

### Chakra

Chakra is the energy source used by ninjas to perform jutsu (techniques). It combines physical and spiritual energy and can be manipulated for:

- Ninjutsu (ninja techniques)
- Genjutsu (illusion techniques)
- Taijutsu (physical combat)
- Medical ninjutsu
