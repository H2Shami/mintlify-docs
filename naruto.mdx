## Overview

Naruto is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

## The series

The franchise consists of two main parts:

- **Naruto (2002-2007)** - Follows Naruto's journey as a young ninja learning the ways of the shinobi
- **Naruto Shippuden (2007-2017)** - Continues the story with an older Naruto facing greater threats

## Key concepts

### Chakra

Chakra is the energy source used by ninjas to perform jutsu (techniques). It combines physical and spiritual energy and can be manipulated for:

- Ninjutsu (ninja techniques)
- Genjutsu (illusion techniques)
- Taijutsu (physical combat)
- Medical ninjutsu

## Ninja ranks

Understanding the ninja hierarchy is essential to following the story:

1. **Academy student** - Young trainees learning basic jutsu and ninja skills
2. **Genin** - Entry-level ninja who go on missions in teams of three with a Jonin leader
3. **Chunin** - Mid-level ninja capable of leading small squads
4. **Jonin** - Elite ninja trusted with the most dangerous missions
5. **Kage** - The leader of a hidden village and its strongest ninja

## Tailed beasts

The tailed beasts (Bijuu) are massive creatures made of chakra, each sealed within a human host called a Jinchuriki.

## Major story arcs

  <Step title="Land of Waves">
