## Overview

Naruto is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village. First serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump in 1999, the series has become one of the best-selling manga of all time with over 250 million copies in circulation.

## The series

The franchise consists of two main parts:

- **Naruto (2002-2007)** - Follows Naruto's journey as a young ninja learning the ways of the shinobi
- **Naruto Shippuden (2007-2017)** - Continues the story with an older Naruto facing greater threats

## Key concepts

### Chakra

Chakra is the energy source used by ninjas to perform jutsu (techniques). It combines physical and spiritual energy and can be manipulated for various combat and utility purposes.

### Jutsu types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ninjutsu" icon="fire">
    Techniques that use chakra to create elemental attacks, clones, and transformations. Includes Naruto's signature Rasengan and Shadow Clone Jutsu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Genjutsu" icon="eye">
    Illusion techniques that manipulate an opponent's senses. The Sharingan and Tsukuyomi are among the most powerful genjutsu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Taijutsu" icon="hand-fist">
    Physical combat techniques that rely on body conditioning rather than chakra. Rock Lee and Might Guy are taijutsu specialists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Senjutsu" icon="tree">
    Sage techniques that draw natural energy from the environment. Mastered by Jiraiya, Naruto, and Hashirama Senju.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Chakra natures

Every ninja has an affinity for one of five basic chakra natures:

| Nature | Element | Notable users |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Fire | Katon | Sasuke Uchiha, Itachi Uchiha |
| Wind | Futon | Naruto Uzumaki, Temari |
| Lightning | Raiton | Kakashi Hatake, Sasuke Uchiha |
| Earth | Doton | Hashirama Senju, Onoki |
| Water | Suiton | Tobirama Senju, Kisame Hoshigaki |

### The Akatsuki

The Akatsuki is a criminal organization of S-rank rogue ninja seeking to capture the tailed beasts.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Key members">
    - **Pain (Nagato)** - Leader who wields the Rinnegan and the Six Paths of Pain
    - **Itachi Uchiha** - Tragic hero who massacred his clan to prevent a coup
    - **Kisame Hoshigaki** - The "Tailless Tailed Beast" with a sentient sword, Samehada
    - **Deidara** - Explosive artist who believes art is fleeting
    - **Sasori** - Puppet master who turned himself into a human puppet
    - **Obito Uchiha** - The masked man behind much of the series' conflict
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Their goal">
    The Akatsuki's public goal is world peace through domination. Their plan involves capturing all nine tailed beasts to power a weapon that can end all conflict, though the true mastermind has deeper motivations tied to the Infinite Tsukuyomi.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### The legendary Sannin

Three legendary ninja trained by the Third Hokage:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Jiraiya, the Toad Sage">
    Naruto's mentor and godfather. A spymaster, author, and one of the strongest ninja in history. His death at the hands of Pain is one of the series' most emotional moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tsunade, the Slug Princess">
    The Fifth Hokage and greatest medical ninja. She overcame her fear of blood to protect the village and mentored Sakura.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Orochimaru, the Snake">
    A rogue ninja obsessed with immortality and learning every jutsu. Serves as one of the primary antagonists in the first half of the series.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The tailed beasts

The tailed beasts (Biju) are massive creatures made of chakra, each with a different number of tails from one to nine.

| Beast | Tails | Jinchuriki | Nature |

| Isobu | Three | Yagura | Water |
| Son Goku | Four | Roshi | Lava |

| Saiken | Six | Utakata | Acid |
