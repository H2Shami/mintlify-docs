## Overview

Naruto is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who seeks recognition from his peers and dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

## The series

The story is divided into two main parts:

### Part I: Naruto (1999-2005)

Follows Naruto's adventures as a Genin in Team 7 alongside Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno, under the guidance of Kakashi Hatake. Major arcs include the Chunin Exams and Sasuke's defection to Orochimaru.

### Part II: Naruto Shippuden (2005-2014)

Set two and a half years later, Naruto returns from training with Jiraiya to confront the Akatsuki organization and unravel the mysteries surrounding the Tailed Beasts and the Fourth Great Ninja War.

## Chakra and ninjutsu

Chakra is the energy that ninja use to perform techniques. It comes from combining physical and spiritual energy.

## The five great nations

Each hidden village is led by a Kage:

- **Konohagakure** (Hidden Leaf) — Hokage
- **Sunagakure** (Hidden Sand) — Kazekage
- **Kirigakure** (Hidden Mist) — Mizukage
- **Kumogakure** (Hidden Cloud) — Raikage
- **Iwagakure** (Hidden Stone) — Tsuchikage
