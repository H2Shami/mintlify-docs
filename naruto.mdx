## Overview

Naruto is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village. First serialized in 1999, the franchise has grown into one of the most popular anime and manga series worldwide.

## The series

The franchise consists of two main parts:

- **Naruto (2002-2007)** - Follows Naruto's journey as a young ninja learning the ways of the shinobi
- **Naruto Shippuden (2007-2017)** - Continues the story with an older Naruto facing greater threats

### Major story arcs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Land of Waves">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Chunin Exams">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sasuke Retrieval">
    Sasuke leaves the Hidden Leaf to join Orochimaru. Naruto and his friends attempt to bring him back, leading to an emotional battle at the Valley of the End.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pain's Assault">
    The Akatsuki leader Pain attacks the Hidden Leaf Village. Naruto returns as a hero and faces his greatest challenge yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fourth Great Ninja War">
    The five great nations unite against Madara Uchiha and the Ten-Tails. Naruto and Sasuke face the ultimate threat to the shinobi world.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

### Chakra

Chakra is the energy source used by ninjas to perform jutsu (techniques). It combines physical and spiritual energy and can be manipulated for:

- Ninjutsu (ninja techniques)
- Genjutsu (illusion techniques)
- Taijutsu (physical combat)
- Medical ninjutsu

### Chakra natures

Every shinobi has an affinity for one or more chakra natures:

| Nature | Element | Notable users |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Fire | Katon | Sasuke Uchiha, Itachi Uchiha |
| Wind | Futon | Naruto Uzumaki, Temari |
| Lightning | Raiton | Kakashi Hatake, Sasuke Uchiha |
| Earth | Doton | Hashirama Senju, Onoki |
| Water | Suiton | Kisame Hoshigaki, Tobirama Senju |

### Tailed beasts

The tailed beasts (Bijuu) are massive creatures made of chakra, each sealed within a human host called a jinchuriki.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="One-Tails to Five-Tails">
    - **Shukaku (One-Tails)** - Sealed within Gaara of the Sand
    - **Matatabi (Two-Tails)** - A cat-like beast made of blue fire
    - **Isobu (Three-Tails)** - A giant turtle that lives in water
    - **Son Goku (Four-Tails)** - A massive ape that uses lava
    - **Kokuo (Five-Tails)** - A dolphin-horse hybrid with incredible speed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Six-Tails to Nine-Tails">
    - **Saiken (Six-Tails)** - A slug that produces corrosive substances
    - **Chomei (Seven-Tails)** - A giant insect capable of flight
    - **Gyuki (Eight-Tails)** - An ox-cephalopod sealed within Killer Bee
    - **Kurama (Nine-Tails)** - The strongest tailed beast, sealed within Naruto Uzumaki
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### The Akatsuki

The Akatsuki is a criminal organization of S-rank rogue ninjas who seek to capture all nine tailed beasts. Members wear black cloaks with red clouds and operate in pairs.
