## Overview

Naruto is a series about a young ninja seeking acceptance from the people around him and the adversities he faces along the way. Created by Masashi Kishimoto, it has grown into one of the most popular anime and manga franchises worldwide.

## The Naruto series

The story spans three different but related series, each following a different generation of ninja in the Hidden Leaf Village.

### Naruto (1997-2005)

The original series introduces Naruto Uzumaki as a young, mischievous ninja-in-training. Episodes follow his early missions, his rivalry with Sasuke Uchiha, and his bond with teammate Sakura Haruno under the guidance of their teacher, Kakashi Hatake. Start here if you are new to the franchise.

### Naruto Shippuden (2006-2014)

Set two and a half years after the original, _Shippuden_ picks up with an older Naruto returning from training. The series focuses on the threat of the Akatsuki organization and culminates in the Fourth Great Ninja War. Watch this after completing the original series.

### Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (2015-Current)

_Boruto_ follows Naruto's son, Boruto Uzumaki, in a more modern Hidden Leaf Village. The series mixes traditional ninja themes with new technology and introduces a fresh generation of characters. Start here only after finishing _Shippuden_, as it builds on earlier story arcs.

## Ninja ranks

The shinobi world has a structured ranking system that determines a ninja's responsibilities and missions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Academy student">
    Young aspiring ninjas who learn the fundamentals of chakra control, basic jutsu, and combat at the Ninja Academy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Genin">
    Entry-level ninja who have graduated from the Academy. They form three-person squads led by a Jonin sensei and take on D-rank and C-rank missions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chunin">
    Mid-level ninja promoted through the Chunin Exams. They can lead small teams and take on B-rank missions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jonin">
    Elite ninja capable of handling A-rank missions. Many serve as team leaders or sensei to Genin squads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kage">
    The strongest ninja in each village who serves as its military and political leader. The five Kage lead the Hidden Leaf, Sand, Mist, Cloud, and Stone villages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Major story arcs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Chunin Exams">
    Team 7 enters the Chunin Exams, where Naruto faces powerful opponents like Neji and Gaara. The arc culminates in Orochimaru's invasion of the Hidden Leaf Village.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sasuke retrieval">
    After Sasuke defects to join Orochimaru, Naruto leads a team of Genin on a desperate mission to bring him back — ending in an iconic battle at the Valley of the End.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pain's assault">
    The Akatsuki leader Pain destroys the Hidden Leaf Village in search of Naruto. Naruto returns to defeat Pain and becomes a hero to his village.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fourth Great Ninja War">
    The five great nations unite against the Akatsuki's plan to trap the world in an infinite illusion. Naruto and Sasuke face Madara Uchiha and Kaguya Otsutsuki in the war's climax.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
