## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. The saga began in 1977 and has since expanded into one of the most beloved media franchises in history.

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things. It binds the galaxy together and can be harnessed by those sensitive to it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Light side" icon="sun">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dark side" icon="moon">

  </Accordion>

## Lightsaber colors

Lightsabers come in various colors, each with its own significance:

- **Blue** - Traditionally used by Jedi Guardians
- **Green** - Often used by Jedi Consulars
- **Red** - Used by Sith, created through synthetic crystals
- **Purple** - Rare, famously wielded by Mace Windu
