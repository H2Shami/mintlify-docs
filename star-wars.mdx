## Overview

Star Wars is a multimedia franchise created by George Lucas that began with the 1977 film "Star Wars" (later subtitled "Episode IV: A New Hope"). The saga spans multiple trilogies, standalone films, animated series, books, and games.

## The Skywalker saga

The main film series consists of three trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire strikes back against the Rebels while Luke trains with Yoda
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels launch a final assault on the second Death Star

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Jedi Knights discover young Anakin Skywalker
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin as Anakin grows into a Jedi
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes rises against the First Order
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks Luke Skywalker's help to save the Resistance
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final confrontation with Emperor Palpatine

## The Force

The Force is a metaphysical energy field that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can harness its power:

- **Light side** - Used by Jedi for knowledge and defense
- **Dark side** - Used by Sith for power and domination

<Note>
  "May the Force be with you" is the traditional greeting and farewell among the Jedi and their allies.
</Note>

## Iconic elements

Star Wars introduced many cultural touchstones:

- **Lightsabers** - Elegant weapons for a more civilized age
- **The Millennium Falcon** - The fastest hunk of junk in the galaxy
- **Droids** - R2-D2 and C-3PO became beloved characters
- **The Death Star** - Moon-sized battle station with planet-destroying power
