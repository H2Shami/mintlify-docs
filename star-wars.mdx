## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. The saga follows the battle between the forces of good (the Jedi) and evil (the Sith) across a galaxy far, far away.

## The main trilogies

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

The story of Luke Skywalker's journey from farm boy to Jedi Knight:

- **A New Hope** (1977) - Luke discovers his destiny and joins the Rebel Alliance
- **The Empire Strikes Back** (1980) - The Empire retaliates and dark truths are revealed
- **Return of the Jedi** (1983) - The final confrontation with the Emperor

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

The rise and fall of Anakin Skywalker:

- **The Phantom Menace** (1999) - A young Anakin is discovered on Tatooine
- **Attack of the Clones** (2002) - The Clone Wars begin
- **Revenge of the Sith** (2005) - Anakin becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

A new generation of heroes and villains:

- **The Force Awakens** (2015) - Rey discovers her connection to the Force
- **The Last Jedi** (2017) - The Resistance fights for survival
- **The Rise of Skywalker** (2019) - The Skywalker saga concludes

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things. It binds the galaxy together and can be harnessed by those sensitive to it.

| Aspect | Light Side | Dark Side |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Practitioners | Jedi | Sith |
| Philosophy | Peace, knowledge, serenity | Passion, power, victory |
| Powers | Healing, mind tricks, foresight | Lightning, choking, manipulation |

<Note>
  "May the Force be with you" - A traditional Jedi blessing
</Note>
