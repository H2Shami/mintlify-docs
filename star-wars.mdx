## Introduction

Star Wars is a legendary science fiction franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film in 1977 and has since expanded into a vast multimedia universe encompassing movies, TV series, books, comics, and video games.

## The main trilogies

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to fight the Empire
- **The Empire Strikes Back** - The rebels face devastating losses and shocking revelations
- **Return of the Jedi** - The final confrontation between the Rebellion and the Empire

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **The Phantom Menace** - The discovery of Anakin Skywalker
- **Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin
- **Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin's fall to the dark side

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes emerges
- **The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks training from Luke Skywalker
- **The Rise of Skywalker** - The final battle against the First Order

## Key concepts

### The Force

The Force is an energy field that connects all living things in the galaxy. Force-sensitive individuals can manipulate it to perform extraordinary feats. It has two aspects:

- **Light side** - Associated with peace, knowledge, and serenity
- **Dark side** - Associated with fear, anger, and aggression

### Lightsabers

Lightsabers are the iconic weapons of the Jedi and Sith. They consist of a plasma blade powered by a kyber crystal and come in various colors:

| Color | Association |
|-------|-------------|
| Blue | Jedi Guardian |
| Green | Jedi Consular |
| Red | Sith |
| Purple | Unique to Mace Windu |

## Notable characters

- **Luke Skywalker** - The hero of the original trilogy
- **Darth Vader** - The fallen Jedi turned Sith Lord
- **Yoda** - The wise Jedi Grand Master
- **Princess Leia** - Leader of the Rebel Alliance
- **Han Solo** - The charismatic smuggler
- **Rey** - The protagonist of the sequel trilogy

## May the Force be with you

Star Wars continues to inspire generations with its timeless themes of hope, redemption, and the eternal struggle between good and evil.
