## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. What began as a single film has expanded into a sprawling universe of films, television series, books, comics, video games, and more.

## The Skywalker saga

The main film series consists of three trilogies spanning over four decades.

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates against the Rebellion, and Luke trains with Yoda
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels attempt to destroy the second Death Star while Luke confronts Darth Vader

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Jedi discover young Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin as Anakin grows into a powerful Jedi
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - Rey discovers her Force sensitivity and joins the Resistance
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks Luke Skywalker's help while the Resistance faces the First Order
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final confrontation with Emperor Palpatine

## Standalone films

Beyond the main saga, several standalone films explore different corners of the galaxy.

- **Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (2016)** - A rebel group steals the plans for the Death Star in a daring mission
- **Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018)** - The origin story of Han Solo and how he met Chewbacca and Lando Calrissian

## Lightsaber colors

Lightsabers are the signature weapons of Force users. The color of a lightsaber crystal reflects the wielder's connection to the Force.

| Color | Meaning | Notable wielders |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Blue | Jedi Guardian, focused on combat | Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi |
| Green | Jedi Consular, focused on the Force | Yoda, Luke Skywalker, Qui-Gon Jinn |
| Red | Sith, corrupted kyber crystal | Darth Vader, Darth Maul, Kylo Ren |
| Purple | Rare, balance of light and dark | Mace Windu |
| White | Purified dark side crystal | Ahsoka Tano |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinel | Rey (rebuilt saber) |

## Timeline

The Star Wars timeline uses the Battle of Yavin (the destruction of the first Death Star) as its reference point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The High Republic era (~500-100 BBY)">
    The golden age of the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic. The Jedi are at the height of their power.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The fall of the Republic (32-19 BBY)">
    Palpatine orchestrates the Clone Wars and manipulates the fall of both the Jedi and the Republic, establishing the Galactic Empire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The age of the Empire (19-0 BBY)">
    The Empire rules the galaxy with an iron fist. The Rebel Alliance forms in secret to oppose it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Galactic Civil War (0-5 ABY)">
    From the destruction of the Death Star to the Battle of Endor, the Rebels fight to restore freedom to the galaxy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The New Republic era (5-28 ABY)">
    The New Republic rises from the ashes of the Empire. Din Djarin and Grogu navigate this era.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The rise of the First Order (28-35 ABY)">
    The First Order emerges from the remnants of the Empire, leading to the events of the sequel trilogy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Star Wars continues to expand with new series on Disney+, upcoming films, and content set across different eras of the timeline.
</Note>
