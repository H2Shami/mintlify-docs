## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film in 1977 and has since expanded into a vast multimedia universe.

## The Skywalker saga

The main saga consists of nine episodic films divided into three trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates and Darth Vader reveals a shocking truth
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels launch a final assault on the second Death Star

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Jedi Knights discover young Anakin Skywalker
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes emerges
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks training from Luke Skywalker
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final battle against Emperor Palpatine

## The Force

The Force is a mystical energy field that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into its power.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Light side">
    The light side of the Force is aligned with peace, knowledge, and serenity. Jedi Knights dedicate themselves to mastering the light side.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dark side">
    The dark side of the Force is fueled by emotions like anger, fear, and hatred. The Sith use these emotions to gain power.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
