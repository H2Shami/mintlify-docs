## Overview

Star Wars is a legendary space opera franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film in 1977 and has since expanded into a massive multimedia universe.

## The trilogies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Original trilogy (1977-1983)">
    - **A New Hope** (1977) - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance
    - **The Empire Strikes Back** (1980) - Darth Vader reveals a shocking truth
    - **Return of the Jedi** (1983) - The final battle against the Empire
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)">
    - **The Phantom Menace** (1999) - Anakin Skywalker is discovered
    - **Attack of the Clones** (2002) - The Clone Wars begin
    - **Revenge of the Sith** (2005) - Anakin becomes Darth Vader
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)">
    - **The Force Awakens** (2015) - A new hero, Rey, emerges
    - **The Last Jedi** (2017) - Luke Skywalker returns
    - **The Rise of Skywalker** (2019) - The Skywalker saga concludes
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The Force

The Force is an energy field that gives Jedi and Sith their powers. It has two sides:

- **Light side** - Used by the Jedi for peace and defense
- **Dark side** - Used by the Sith for power and control

<Note>
  "May the Force be with you" is the most iconic phrase from the franchise.
</Note>
