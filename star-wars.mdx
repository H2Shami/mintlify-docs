## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. The saga spans movies, TV series, books, and more, set in a galaxy far, far away.

## The original trilogy

The franchise began with the original trilogy:

- **A New Hope (1977)** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **The Empire Strikes Back (1980)** - The Empire strikes back against the Rebels, revealing shocking truths about Luke's heritage
- **Return of the Jedi (1983)** - The Rebels launch a final assault on the Empire and the second Death Star

## Key concepts

### The Force

The Force is a mystical energy field that connects all living things. Force-sensitive individuals can harness its power for:

- Telekinesis
- Mind tricks
- Enhanced reflexes
- Precognition
