## Overview

Star Wars is a space opera franchise created by George Lucas that began with the 1977 film and has expanded into a massive multimedia universe.

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into this power.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Light side" icon="sun">
    The Jedi use the light side of the Force for knowledge and defense. Key abilities include:
    - Telekinesis
    - Mind tricks
    - Enhanced reflexes
    - Force healing
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dark side" icon="moon">
    The Sith draw power from the dark side through emotion and aggression. Key abilities include:
    - Force lightning
    - Force choke
    - Enhanced strength
    - Manipulation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
