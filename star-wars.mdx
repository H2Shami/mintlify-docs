## Overview

Star Wars is a space opera media franchise created by George Lucas. The franchise began with the eponymous 1977 film and quickly became a worldwide pop culture phenomenon.

## The original trilogy

The original trilogy introduced audiences to the galaxy far, far away:

- **A New Hope (1977)** - Luke Skywalker joins forces with a Jedi Knight, a cocky pilot, a Wookiee, and two droids to save the galaxy from the Empire's world-destroying battle station
- **The Empire Strikes Back (1980)** - After the Rebels are overpowered by the Empire, Luke Skywalker begins Jedi training with Yoda while his friends are pursued across the galaxy
- **Return of the Jedi (1983)** - The Rebels mount an attack on the second Death Star while Luke confronts Darth Vader

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into its power.

### Light side

The light side of the Force is aligned with:
- Compassion
- Selflessness
- Self-knowledge and enlightenment
- Healing and protection

### Dark side

The dark side of the Force feeds on:
- Fear
- Anger
- Hatred
- Aggression
