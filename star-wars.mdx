## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. The franchise began with the original film *Star Wars* (later subtitled *Episode IV – A New Hope*) and has since expanded into a massive multimedia universe.

## The Skywalker Saga

The core of Star Wars consists of three trilogies following the Skywalker family:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV – A New Hope** (1977)
- **Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back** (1980)
- **Episode VI – Return of the Jedi** (1983)

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I – The Phantom Menace** (1999)
- **Episode II – Attack of the Clones** (2002)
- **Episode III – Revenge of the Sith** (2005)

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII – The Force Awakens** (2015)
- **Episode VIII – The Last Jedi** (2017)
- **Episode IX – The Rise of Skywalker** (2019)

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into this power to perform extraordinary feats.
