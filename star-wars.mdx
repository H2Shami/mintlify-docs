## Overview

Star Wars is a multimedia franchise created by George Lucas that began with the 1977 film "Star Wars" (later subtitled "Episode IV: A New Hope"). The franchise has since expanded into one of the most successful entertainment properties in history.

## The saga

The Star Wars saga consists of three main trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to save Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates, and Luke learns a shocking truth about his father
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels launch a final assault on the Empire while Luke confronts Darth Vader

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Young Anakin Skywalker is discovered by the Jedi
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin as Anakin grows closer to Padmé
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin's fall to the dark side and transformation into Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes emerges to fight the First Order
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks out Luke Skywalker while the Resistance faces extinction
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final battle against Emperor Palpatine

## The Force

The Force is a mystical energy field that binds the galaxy together. It has two aspects:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Light side | Used by the Jedi for peace and defense |
| Dark side | Used by the Sith for power and domination |

## Iconic characters

- **Luke Skywalker** - The hero of the original trilogy
- **Darth Vader** - The iconic villain and Luke's father
- **Princess Leia** - Leader of the Rebel Alliance
- **Han Solo** - Smuggler turned hero
- **Yoda** - The wise Jedi Master
- **Obi-Wan Kenobi** - Jedi Knight and mentor to both Anakin and Luke
