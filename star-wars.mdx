## Overview

Star Wars is a legendary space opera franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film in 1977 and has since expanded into a vast multimedia universe spanning films, television series, books, comics, and games.

## The Skywalker saga

The core of Star Wars is the nine-film Skywalker Saga, divided into three trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to fight the Empire
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates and reveals shocking truths
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels make their final stand against the Emperor

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - The discovery of young Anakin Skywalker
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin's fall to the dark side

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes rises
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks training from Luke Skywalker
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final confrontation with the Sith

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds and penetrates everything, binding the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can harness its power for various abilities:

- **Telekinesis** - Moving objects with the mind
- **Mind tricks** - Influencing the thoughts of others
- **Force lightning** - Projecting energy from the fingertips
- **Force healing** - Mending wounds and injuries
