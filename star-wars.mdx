## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. It has become one of the most influential and beloved science fiction franchises in history.

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds and penetrates everything, binding the galaxy together.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Light side">
    The light side of the Force is aligned with:
    - Peace and serenity
    - Knowledge and defense
    - Protection of the innocent
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dark side">
    The dark side is fueled by:
    - Fear and anger
    - Hatred and aggression
    - Desire for power
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
