## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film released in 1977 and has since expanded into a massive universe of films, television series, books, and more.

## The Skywalker Saga

## The Force

The Force is a metaphysical energy field that binds the galaxy together. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into its power to perform extraordinary feats.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Light side" icon="sun">
    The light side of the Force is aligned with calmness, peace, and passivity. The Jedi Order uses the light side to protect and serve the galaxy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dark side" icon="moon">
    The dark side is fueled by emotions like anger, fear, and hatred. The Sith harness its power for personal gain and domination.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
