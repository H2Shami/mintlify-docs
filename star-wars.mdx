## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the original film released in 1977 and has since expanded into a vast multimedia universe.

## The trilogies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Original trilogy (1977-1983)">
    - **Episode IV: A New Hope** (1977)
    - **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** (1980)
    - **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** (1983)
    
    The original trilogy follows Luke Skywalker as he joins the Rebel Alliance to fight against the Galactic Empire and confronts Darth Vader.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)">
    - **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** (1999)
    - **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** (2002)
    - **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** (2005)
    
    The prequel trilogy chronicles the fall of Anakin Skywalker and his transformation into Darth Vader.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="film" title="Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)">
    - **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** (2015)
    - **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** (2017)
    - **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** (2019)
    
    The sequel trilogy introduces new heroes Rey, Finn, and Poe as they battle the First Order.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The Force

The Force is an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds and penetrates everything, binding the galaxy together.

<Tip>
  "May the Force be with you" is one of the most famous quotes from the franchise.
</Tip>

### Force abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Telekinesis | Moving objects with the mind |
| Mind trick | Influencing the thoughts of others |
| Force lightning | Generating electrical energy |
| Force healing | Healing wounds and injuries |

## Iconic locations

- **Tatooine** - A desert planet and Luke Skywalker's home world
- **Coruscant** - The capital of the Galactic Republic and later the Empire
- **Hoth** - An ice planet that served as a Rebel base
- **Endor** - Forest moon home to the Ewoks
- **Dagobah** - Swamp planet where Yoda lived in exile

<Note>
  The Star Wars universe continues to expand with new films, TV series, books, and games.
</Note>
