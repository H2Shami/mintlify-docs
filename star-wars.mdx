## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. The franchise has expanded to include films, television series, books, comics, video games, and more.

## The Skywalker Saga

The main film series consists of three trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates against the Rebellion, and Luke trains with Yoda
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels attempt to destroy the second Death Star while Luke confronts Darth Vader

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Jedi discover young Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin as Anakin grows into a powerful Jedi
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - Rey discovers her Force sensitivity and joins the Resistance
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks Luke Skywalker's help while the Resistance faces the First Order
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final confrontation with Emperor Palpatine

## Lightsaber colors

Lightsaber colors reflect the wielder's connection to the Force and their role:

| Color | Meaning | Notable wielders |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Blue | Jedi Guardian, focused on combat | Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi |
| Green | Jedi Consular, focused on the Force | Yoda, Luke Skywalker, Qui-Gon Jinn |
| Red | Sith, powered by synthetic or corrupted crystals | Darth Vader, Darth Maul, Kylo Ren |
| Purple | Rare, balance of light and dark | Mace Windu |
| White | Purified dark side crystals | Ahsoka Tano |

## Timeline

The Star Wars timeline spans thousands of years. Here is a simplified view of the major eras:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The High Republic era">
    Set roughly 200 years before the prequel trilogy. The Jedi Order is at its peak, exploring the outer reaches of the galaxy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The prequel era">
    The fall of the Republic, the Clone Wars, and the rise of the Empire. Anakin Skywalker becomes Darth Vader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The original trilogy era">
    The Galactic Civil War between the Rebel Alliance and the Empire. Luke Skywalker redeems Darth Vader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The sequel era">
    The rise of the First Order and the Resistance. Rey confronts Emperor Palpatine and restores balance to the Force.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Star Wars universe spans films, TV series, books, comics, and video games, making it one of the largest fictional universes ever created.
</Note>
