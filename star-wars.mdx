## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. The franchise has expanded to include films, television series, books, comics, video games, and more.

## The Skywalker Saga

The main film series consists of three trilogies:

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates against the Rebellion, and Luke trains with Yoda
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The Rebels attempt to destroy the second Death Star while Luke confronts Darth Vader

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - Jedi discover young Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin as Anakin grows into a powerful Jedi
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - Rey discovers her Force sensitivity and joins the Resistance
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks Luke Skywalker's help while the Resistance faces the First Order
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final confrontation with Emperor Palpatine

## Lightsaber colors

Lightsaber crystals determine the blade's color and reflect the wielder's connection to the Force.

| Color | Meaning | Notable wielders |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Blue | Guardian, combat-focused Jedi | Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi |
| Green | Consular, wisdom-focused Jedi | Yoda, Qui-Gon Jinn, Luke Skywalker |
| Red | Sith, corrupted kyber crystal | Darth Vader, Darth Maul, Kylo Ren |
| Purple | Balance of light and dark | Mace Windu |
| White | Purified dark side crystal | Ahsoka Tano |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinel | Rey, Jedi Temple Guards |

## Television series

Star Wars has expanded significantly through television, especially on Disney+.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Animated series">
    - **Star Wars: The Clone Wars (2008-2020)** - Fills the gap between Episodes II and III, following Anakin and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano
    - **Star Wars Rebels (2014-2018)** - Set before Episode IV, follows a small rebel crew fighting the Empire
    - **Star Wars: The Bad Batch (2021-2024)** - Follows a squad of elite clone troopers after the fall of the Republic
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Live-action series">
    - **The Mandalorian (2019-present)** - A lone bounty hunter navigates the outer reaches of the galaxy after the fall of the Empire
    - **Andor (2022-present)** - A spy thriller set five years before Rogue One, following Cassian Andor's path to the Rebellion
    - **Ahsoka (2023-present)** - Ahsoka Tano investigates a growing threat to the galaxy after the Empire's fall
    - **Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022)** - Set between Episodes III and IV, follows Obi-Wan watching over young Luke on Tatooine
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
