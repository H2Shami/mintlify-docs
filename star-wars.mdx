## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas in 1977. The saga spans multiple trilogies, spin-offs, and an expanded universe of stories.

## The trilogies

### Original trilogy (1977-1983)

- **Episode IV: A New Hope** - Luke Skywalker joins the Rebel Alliance to destroy the Death Star
- **Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back** - The Empire retaliates and reveals shocking truths
- **Episode VI: Return of the Jedi** - The final confrontation between the Rebels and the Empire

### Prequel trilogy (1999-2005)

- **Episode I: The Phantom Menace** - The discovery of Anakin Skywalker
- **Episode II: Attack of the Clones** - The Clone Wars begin
- **Episode III: Revenge of the Sith** - Anakin's fall to the dark side

### Sequel trilogy (2015-2019)

- **Episode VII: The Force Awakens** - A new generation of heroes emerges
- **Episode VIII: The Last Jedi** - Rey seeks training from Luke Skywalker
- **Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker** - The final battle against the Sith

## The Force

The Force is an energy field that connects all living things. Force-sensitive individuals can tap into its power:

- **Light side** - Used by the Jedi for peace and defense
- **Dark side** - Used by the Sith for power and control

```
"May the Force be with you." - Common Jedi farewell
```
