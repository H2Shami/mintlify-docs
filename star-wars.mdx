## Overview

Star Wars is an epic space opera media franchise created by George Lucas. The franchise began with the release of the original film in 1977 and has since expanded into a vast universe of movies, television series, books, comics, and more.

## The Force

The Force is a metaphysical and ubiquitous power in the Star Wars universe. It is wielded by Force-sensitive individuals, most notably the Jedi and the Sith.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Light side" icon="sun">
    The light side of the Force is aligned with peace, knowledge, serenity, and selflessness. The Jedi Order follows the light side, using the Force for defense and protection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dark side" icon="moon">
    The dark side of the Force is fueled by emotions like anger, fear, and hatred. The Sith embrace the dark side to gain power and domination.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
