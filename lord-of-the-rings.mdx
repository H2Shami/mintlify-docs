## Overview

The Lord of the Rings is an epic high-fantasy novel written by J.R.R. Tolkien, originally published in three volumes from 1954 to 1955. Set in the world of Middle-earth, the story follows the quest to destroy the One Ring and defeat the Dark Lord Sauron.

## The trilogy

The main story is divided into three volumes:

### The Fellowship of the Ring (1954)

Frodo Baggins inherits the One Ring from his uncle Bilbo and learns of its dark origins. He sets out from the Shire with a fellowship of nine companions to destroy the Ring in the fires of Mount Doom.

### The Two Towers (1954)

The fellowship is broken. Frodo and Sam continue toward Mordor with the treacherous Gollum as their guide, while Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli pursue the Uruk-hai who captured Merry and Pippin.

### The Return of the King (1955)

The forces of good and evil clash in the final battle for Middle-earth. Aragorn claims his throne, while Frodo and Sam make their final approach to Mount Doom.

## Key locations

- **The Shire** - Peaceful homeland of the Hobbits
- **Rivendell** - Elvish refuge ruled by Lord Elrond
- **Moria** - Ancient Dwarven kingdom beneath the Misty Mountains
- **Lothlórien** - Realm of the Lady Galadriel
- **Rohan** - Kingdom of the horse-lords
- **Gondor** - Greatest kingdom of Men in the Third Age
- **Mordor** - Dark land of Sauron, where the One Ring must be destroyed
